Remember: a reframe isn't meant to argue with something painful that really happened, or to make a feeling go away. It's just a second way of looking at a thought, so you have a choice. If a belief is tied to something heavy, or you're having thoughts of harming yourself, please reach out to someone you trust or a mental health professional. In the U.S., you can call or text 988 any time. And if setting a boundary or speaking up doesn't feel safe with someone in your life, trust that feeling. The National Domestic Violence Hotline (1-800-799-7233) can help you think it through.
